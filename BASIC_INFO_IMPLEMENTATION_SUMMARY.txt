FORM FIELDS
-----------
Required:
  • Sex (dropdown: Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say)
  • Date of Birth (date picker)
  • Age (dropdown 16-99, auto-fills from DOB)
  • Complete Address (textarea)
  • Province (dropdown, 81 provinces)
  • City/Municipality (dropdown, filtered by province)
  • Senior High School Strand (dropdown: ABM, STEM, HUMSS, TVL, Others)
  • Senior High School Name (text input)
  
Optional:
  • Civil Status (dropdown: Single, Married, Widowed, Separated, Divorced)
  
Conditional:
  • Strand Specification (text input, required when "Others" is selected)
