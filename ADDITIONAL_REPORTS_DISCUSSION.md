# 📊 Additional Reports Section - Discussion Guide

## 🎯 Purpose
This document helps you decide what reports to create from the student basic information data. We've removed the old Additional Reports section and need to replace it with meaningful reports based on the information students provide.

---

## 📋 What Data Do We Have?

When students enter the exam, they provide:

### Personal Info
- Sex (Male, Female, Other, Prefer not to say)
- Date of Birth / Age
- Civil Status (optional)
- Applicant Type (New College Applicant, Transferee, ALS passer)
- PWD Status (Yes, No, Prefer not to answer)

### Location Info
- Complete Address
- City/Municipality
- Province (81 provinces available)

### Education Info
- Senior High School Strand (ABM, STEM, HUMSS, TVL, Others)
- Senior High School Name

### Plus We Can Combine With:
- Exam Scores
- Interview Scores
- Overall Admission Rating
- Admission Status

---

## 🤔 What Reports Would Be Useful?

Here are **11 report ideas** organized by category. Please review and tell us which ones would be most valuable:

### 🗺️ **Geographic Reports** (Understanding Where Applicants Come From)

#### 1. **Geographic Distribution Report**
**What it shows**: 
- How many applicants come from each province
- Top cities/municipalities
- Geographic spread of applicants

**Why useful**: 
- Know your geographic reach
- Plan outreach programs
- Identify areas with many applicants

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Most likely useful)

---

#### 2. **Geographic Performance Report**
**What it shows**: 
- Which provinces/cities have highest-performing students
- Average exam scores by location
- Performance patterns by geography

**Why useful**: 
- Identify high-performing regions
- Understand geographic performance differences
- Plan regional programs

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

---

### 🎓 **Educational Background Reports** (Understanding Student Preparation)

#### 3. **Strand Distribution Report**
**What it shows**: 
- How many applicants from each SHS strand (ABM, STEM, HUMSS, TVL, Others)
- Percentage breakdown
- Strand trends over time

**Why useful**: 
- Understand educational backgrounds
- Plan curriculum alignment
- See which strands students come from

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Very useful for planning)

---

#### 4. **Strand Performance Analysis**
**What it shows**: 
- Which strands perform best on exams
- Average scores by strand
- Strand vs. admission rates

**Why useful**: 
- Understand which educational backgrounds prepare students better
- Identify curriculum alignment needs
- Guide admission strategy

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

---

#### 5. **Top Schools Report**
**What it shows**: 
- Which high schools send the most applicants
- School performance rankings
- Schools with highest admission rates

**Why useful**: 
- Identify partnership opportunities
- Understand feeder schools
- Plan outreach to specific schools

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐

---

### 👥 **Demographic Reports** (Understanding Who Applies)

#### 6. **Demographic Overview Report**
**What it shows**: 
- Age distribution
- Gender breakdown
- Civil status
- Applicant type (New, Transferee, ALS)
- PWD statistics

**Why useful**: 
- Understand applicant demographics
- Plan for diversity
- Resource allocation

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

---

#### 7. **Age Distribution Analysis**
**What it shows**: 
- Age ranges of applicants
- Average age by course
- Age vs. performance

**Why useful**: 
- Understand typical applicant age
- Identify age trends

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐

---

#### 8. **Applicant Type Performance Report**
**What it shows**: 
- Performance of New College Applicants vs. Transferees vs. ALS passers
- Admission rates by type
- Comparison charts

**Why useful**: 
- Understand different applicant groups
- Tailor support programs
- Compare performance

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐

---

#### 9. **PWD Statistics Report**
**What it shows**: 
- PWD representation
- PWD vs. Non-PWD performance
- PWD admission rates
- Geographic distribution of PWD applicants

**Why useful**: 
- Ensure accessibility compliance
- Understand PWD applicant needs
- Plan support services

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐

---

### 📊 **Comprehensive Reports** (Big Picture Analysis)

#### 10. **Comprehensive Applicant Profile Report**
**What it shows**: 
- Everything combined: demographics + geography + education + performance
- Cross-analysis (strand vs. geography, age vs. performance)
- Key insights and recommendations

**Why useful**: 
- Complete picture for stakeholders
- Annual reports
- Strategic planning

**Priority**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

---

#### 11. **Completion Rate Analysis**
**What it shows**: 
- How many students complete the basic info form
- Completion rates by province, strand, etc.
- Drop-off analysis

**Why useful**: 
- Understand form completion patterns
- Identify issues in the process
- Improve user experience

**Priority**: ⭐⭐

---

## 🎯 Recommended Starting Point

Based on typical stakeholder needs, I recommend starting with these **3 reports**:

1. **Geographic Distribution Report** - Most requested, very useful
2. **Strand Distribution Report** - Essential for educational planning
3. **Demographic Overview Report** - General insights needed

Then add performance reports as Phase 2.

**Report**: Strand Distribution Report

### Use Case 3: Performance Analysis
**Need**: "Do STEM students perform better than other strands? We want to see the data."

**Report**: Top Schools Report
